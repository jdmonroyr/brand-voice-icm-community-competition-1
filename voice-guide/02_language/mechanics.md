# Mechanics

## Sentence rhythm
Short sentences carry the calm. Vary length, but default short. One idea per sentence. Avoid subordinate clauses stacked more than once ("which means that, because of this, we also...").

## Contractions
Always. "We'll," "you're," "it's." Full forms read stiff.

## Punctuation
- Periods over exclamation points. One exclamation point per piece of copy, maximum, and only where genuine.
- Em dashes are fine for an aside — used the way this sentence uses one.
- No ALL CAPS for emphasis. No excessive ellipses.

## Emoji
Sparingly. A single 🐾 is acceptable at the end of a caption, never mid-sentence, never stacked, never in an email. Default to none; add one only if the specific piece calls for it.

## Length
- Captions: 2–4 sentences. Say one thing.
- Emails: as short as the situation allows. A one-paragraph apology beats a three-paragraph one.

## Formatting
- Captions: no headers, no bullet lists — it's a spoken-voice medium.
- Emails: greeting by first name, short paragraphs, plain sign-off ("— Dana" or "— The Ruff Cuts team"), no "Best regards," no signature block with taglines.
