# Competitor mentions

## When this applies
Any copy that draws a comparison to traditional grooming salons, kennel-based groomers, or the grooming industry generally — website copy, FAQ answers, sales conversations written down.

## Rules
- Never name a specific competitor.
- Never disparage the *people* — most traditional groomers are good at their jobs. The contrast is about the *model* (kennel, car ride, other dogs, drop-off), not skill.
- State the tradeoff, don't sell against it. Let the reader draw the conclusion.
- One comparison per piece of copy, stated once. Don't return to it three times in the same piece.

## Worked example

FAQ answer to "How is this different from a regular groomer?":

> Most groomers are good at what they do — this isn't about that. The difference is what happens around the actual grooming: the car ride, the kennel, the other dogs, being dropped off and picked up hours later. We skip all of that. Your dog gets groomed in your driveway and never leaves it. If your dog does fine with the car-and-kennel version, there's no reason to switch. If it stresses your dog out — or you — this is the alternative.
