# Apology / service recovery email

## When this applies
We were late, had to reschedule, made a mistake on the groom, or otherwise fell short of what we told the customer to expect.

## Rules
- Open with the apology, not context. Don't make them read three sentences of explanation before they get "I'm sorry."
- State plainly what happened. One sentence. No blame-shifting ("due to unforeseen circumstances beyond our control").
- Say what we're doing about it — concretely, not "we're looking into it."
- Offer the fix before they have to ask for one.
- Close warm, not formal. No "Best regards" or signature block.
- Keep it to one short paragraph plus the fix. Long apologies read as anxious, not sincere.

## Worked example

Situation: van ran 40 minutes behind schedule with no heads-up text.

> Hi Maria,
>
> Sorry about this morning — we ran behind and didn't text you until we were already late, which isn't how we do things. That's on us.
>
> I've moved Biscuit to the front of the list for his next groom, no charge, whenever you're ready to book. And I've fixed the scheduling gap that caused this so it doesn't happen again.
>
> Thanks for the patience today.
>
> — Dana
