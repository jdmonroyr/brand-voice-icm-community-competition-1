# Social captions

## When this applies
Instagram/Facebook captions accompanying a photo or video of a groom, a van, or a dog.

## Rules
- Lead with the dog, not the business. The photo is the hook; the caption adds one thought.
- 2–4 sentences. One idea. No listicle format, no hashtag walls (2–3 relevant hashtags max, if any).
- It's fine to be a little proud of the work — never fine to be salesy in a caption. Save the pitch for the bio and the website.
- Use the dog's name if known.

## Worked examples

**A finished groom photo:**
> Winnie got the full treatment this morning — bath, trim, the works — without ever leaving her driveway. This is the whole job, honestly. 🐾

**Announcing availability:**
> We've got a few Saturday slots open next month. If your dog's overdue and the idea of a kennel drop-off makes you both anxious, this is the alternative.

**Behind the scenes (van interior):**
> This is the entire grooming setup — tub, dryer, table, one very good boy. No waiting room, because there isn't one.
