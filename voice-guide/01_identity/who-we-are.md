# Who we are

## The business
Ruff Cuts is a mobile dog grooming service in Austin. We drive a fully-equipped van to your house, groom your dog inside it, and leave. No kennels, no drop-offs, no waiting room.

Started by Dana two years ago, after 15 years as a vet tech watching dogs shake and stress out in clinic environments. The whole business exists because she thought there had to be a better way. Now it's Dana plus two other groomers, three vans, booked six weeks out.

## The customer
Dual-income households, 35–55. They have money, not time. A lot of them work from home. They pay $95–$140 for a groom because it means they never leave the house or rearrange their day — we show up, we text when we're done, they bring the dog inside. That's the whole interaction.

They are not people who need convincing that grooming matters. They need convincing that *this version* of grooming is worth paying a premium for. Talk to them like the smart, busy adults they are.

## What makes us different
Not cheaper. Not fancier. Just: the dog never leaves the driveway. No car ride, no kennel, no other dogs, no stranger holding the leash while you're not there. For an anxious dog — or an anxious owner — that's the entire pitch.

## The stance in one line
The friend who happens to be a professional groomer. Someone you trust with your dog because they clearly know what they're doing, and who never makes it weird about it.
