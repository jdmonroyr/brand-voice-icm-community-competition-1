# CLIENT BRIEF

## Weekly Competition #1: The Fake Client

## THE CLIENT

My name's Dana. I run a mobile dog grooming van called 'Ruff Cuts' out of Austin. We come to your house, groom your dog in the van, and leave. No kennels. No drop-offs. No anxiety for the dog or the owner.

I started this two years ago after 15 years as a vet tech. I was burned out watching dogs shake and stress in clinic environments. Figured there had to be a better way. Now it's me and two other groomers, three vans, and we're fully booked six weeks out.

Our customers are mostly dual-income households, 35-55, who have the money but not the time. They'll pay $95-$140 for a groom because they never have to leave their house or rearrange their day. A lot of them work from home. We show up, we text when we're done, they bring the dog inside. That's it.

## THE PROBLEM

Here's my problem: I need to hire someone to handle our social media and customer emails, but every time I try to explain how we talk to people, I can't get it right. We're not cutesy. We don't do 'fur baby' or 'pawsitively' or any of that. But we're also not stiff. We're warm, we're professionals, we genuinely love dogs. We talk to our customers like smart adults, not like they're in a children's book.

## WHAT I NEED

I need a full brand voice guide I can hand to anyone who writes for us. Tone, phrases we'd use, phrases we'd never use, how we talk about competitors (we don't trash them, but we're clearly different), how we handle complaints, how we write a caption vs. how we write an apology email. All of it.

## THE VIBE

Your friend who happens to be an expert. Someone you trust with your dog because they clearly know what they're doing, but who doesn't make it weird. Premium but not snobby. Professional but not corporate. We're the people your dog is actually happy to see.

## YOUR DELIVERABLE

A complete brand voice document for Ruff Cuts. How you structure it, how detailed you go, what sections you include, how you get AI to nail the tone... that's all on you.
